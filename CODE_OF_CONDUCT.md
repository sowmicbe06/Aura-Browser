# Aura Browser Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Aura Browser community aims to be welcoming, respectful, and helpful to everyone, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent and discuss disagreements calmly.
- Keep discussions relevant and useful.
- Respect other people's privacy.
- Share only content they have permission to share.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insults, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Spam, scams, or repeated off-topic promotion.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Posting passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.
- Misusing issue reports or discussions to target individuals.

## Moderation

The project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from community spaces.

## Reporting a concern

To report a conduct concern, contact the project maintainer privately through a verified contact method listed in the official repository or project website. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Scope

This code applies to Aura Browser project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Discussions, and pull-request conversations.

Thank you for helping keep the Aura Browser community welcoming.
